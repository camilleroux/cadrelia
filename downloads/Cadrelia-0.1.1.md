# Cadrelia 0.1.1

Fixes from the first days of testing.

- Animated GIFs now play on the desktop, like videos (a "GIF" badge marks them).
- Interactive hic et nunc works show their own cover image instead of a black disc.
- Changing the framing of the artwork on screen no longer swaps it for another one.
- The menu bar has a **Restart** button to start a live artwork again from the beginning, and says why an
  artwork set to move stays still (turned, on battery, Low Power Mode…).
- The framing shown in an artwork's details is now the one actually used on the desktop.
- The site moved to cadrelia.camilleroux.com.

Something wrong or confusing? **Send Feedback…** in the menu bar.
