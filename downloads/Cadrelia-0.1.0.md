# Cadrelia 0.1.0: first test version

Thanks for trying Cadrelia! This is an early test version: things may change or break.

- Add Ethereum, Tezos or Bitcoin wallets and see your collection in the Library.
- Hang your artworks on the desktop, live or as still images, with Fit, Fill or Mat framing.
- Screen saver, labels, and exhibitions, frames and label styles for collectors.

Something wrong or confusing? **Send Feedback…** in the menu bar.
